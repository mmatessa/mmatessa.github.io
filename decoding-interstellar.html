Algorithms for Decoding Interstellar Messages

Michael Matessa

Journal of the British Interplanetary Society, volume 75, issue 10, pages 357–361, October 2022.

Read the full paper (PDF)

Abstract

How would we determine the meaning of an interstellar message from a distant civilization? This paper describes algorithms for assigning meaning to symbols found in such a message. It is assumed the message is pre-parsed, resulting in symbols grouped into expressions. Algorithms assign meanings to symbols within an expression and meta-algorithms maintain a list of symbol meanings across expressions. The algorithms described in this paper are Equality, Counting, Function, Base, Approximation, Constants, and Patterns. The meta-algorithms are Intersect, Prune, and Substitute. These algorithms have been implemented in software, and results from a sample message are given that assign meanings ranging from numbers to the concepts of mass and length. By developing algorithms, it is hoped that message decoding can grow from an art done by individuals to a science done with algorithms.
